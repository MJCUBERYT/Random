PLL Training

Improve recognition, finger tricks and execution across your PLL algorithms.

Focus:
• Recognize the case before turning.
• Use consistent finger tricks.
• Keep your hands relaxed.
• Practise difficult cases separately.
🟨

OLL Training

Build faster recognition and smoother last-layer execution.

Focus:
• Learn to identify cases from their patterns.
• Practise awkward finger tricks slowly first.
• Aim for reliable execution before maximum speed.
• Review cases you frequently confuse.
👁️

Blind Cross

Plan your cross during inspection, then execute it without looking at the cube.

Focus:
• Trace the movement of each cross piece.
• Visualize where pieces move after every turn.
• Start with a reliable solution, then optimize it.
• Stop and re-plan if your visualization becomes unclear.
🔍

F2L Lookahead

Improve your ability to track upcoming pairs while finishing the current pair.

Focus:
• Turn slowly enough to see the next pair.
• Avoid unnecessary pauses between pairs.
• Track pieces rather than staring at one location.
• Prioritize continuous turning over raw TPS.
🔄

Rotationless F2L

Practice having maximum 2 cube rotations during f2l using efficient solutions.

Focus:
• Practise R, U and L-based solutions where useful.
• Learn to recognize pairs on the back and left sides.
• Use efficient solutions without forcing awkward ones.
• Save difficult cases for deliberate practice.
🎓

Learn From the Best

Watch walkthrough solves and study techniques used by experienced speedcubers.

Focus:
• Watch how advanced solvers plan their cross.
• Look for efficient F2L solutions.
• Study lookahead and recognition techniques.
• Test useful ideas in your own solves.
