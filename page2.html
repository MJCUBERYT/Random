TRAINING MODULES

PLL Mastery 01 / ALGORITHMS

Train every PLL 30 times daily. Improve recognition, finger tricks and execution, aiming for sub-1-second execution.

Training tips
Recognition: Learn to identify cases from their patterns.
Finger tricks: Find comfortable, repeatable finger movements.
Execution: Aim for smooth, accurate algorithms instead of forcing speed.

OLL Mastery 02 / ALGORITHMS

Train every OLL 10 times daily, improving recognition and aiming for sub-2-second execution.

Training tips
Recognition: Notice the important sticker patterns.
Finger tricks: Practise comfortable grips and efficient movements.
Execution: Build consistency before pushing for speed.

Blind Cross 03 / PLANNING

Inspect the cross, then solve it blindfolded 20 times daily, aiming for under 2 seconds.

Training tips
Inspection: Plan the cross before starting.
Memory: Remember the required moves and piece positions.
Execution: Keep the cube orientation stable and practise accurately.

Blind F2L 04 / LOOKAHEAD

Practise F2L pairs blindfolded to improve piece tracking, memory and lookahead.

Training tips
Start slowly: Memorise the pair's positions before closing your eyes.
Track: Follow the pieces mentally through the moves.
Apply: Return to normal solves and practise finding the next pair during execution.

Rotationless F2L 05 / EO

Practise solving pairs at the back and on the left without unnecessary cube rotations.

Training tips
Keep orientation: Practise using R, U and L moves where suitable.
Recognise: Learn to spot pairs without turning the cube.
EO strategy: Handle awkward edges strategically rather than forcing inefficient solutions.

Walkthrough Solves 06 / LEARNING

Follow expert walkthroughs and learn efficient solutions, finger tricks and new techniques.

Training tips
Cross: Study how the solver plans and executes it.
F2L: Notice pair choices and how the next pair is located.
Review: Reproduce useful solutions and adapt them to your own turning style.
