MJCUBER Training


Time every PLL 30 times everyday until great recognition and under 1 second.


Time every OLL 10 times everyday until great recognition under 2 seconds.


Inspect cross in inspection and solve the cross while blindfolded 20 times everyday until under 2 seconds.


Time every f2l pair blindfolded a few times everyday until every pair is under 1 second.


Practice for solving pairs in the back or the left side everyday until normal.


Watch walkthrough solves and get tips and tricks from world champions or great solvers everyday.
